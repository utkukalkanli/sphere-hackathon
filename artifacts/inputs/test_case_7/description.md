# Angled‑bore mounting bracket (practice piece)

**Type / function:** A single‑piece bracket that combines a horizontal top mounting flange (with a central domed boss) and a lower, in‑plane‑rotated flat plate carrying a large stepped bore, joined by curved side ribs.

**Envelope:** 162.25 x 95.15 x 152.74 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 327.3 cm³; 10 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is built up from three main elements in a single solid. At the top (Z ≈ 136–153 mm) is a horizontal rectangular mounting flange lying in the X–Y plane, with four rounded corner lugs and a rectangular pocket recessed into its top face. From the centre of this flange a short vertical, dome‑topped boss rises, and a vertical web/neck descends to a large flat plate at the bottom of the part. That lower plate has its face normal along Y (so its bores run in Y), but its rounded‑rectangular outline is rotated about 20° in its own plane, giving the tilted appearance in the front view. Curved gusset ribs on the left and right tie the flange to the lower body. The part is roughly symmetric about the X = 81 mm mid‑plane for the flange and ribs, but the lower plate and its holes are not symmetric.

## Features
- **Top flange lugs (4 vertical Z through‑holes):** two Ø11 holes at (X=43.65, Y=85.8) and (X=113.95, Y=85.8) on the +Y edge, and two Ø9.5 holes at (X=9.45, Y=32.05) and (X=152.25, Y=32.05) on the side edges; all go straight through the flange.
- **Top flange pocket:** a rectangular recess open on the top face, bounded inside the lug ring, with a flat floor and a small central raised pad (no separate bore).
- **Top central domed boss:** a short vertical boss at the flange centre (X≈85, Y≈46.5) with a spherical/domed top. It carries a horizontal (Y) stepped through‑bore: Ø17.5 through (≈75 mm long), stepped on the −Y side to Ø21 (2 mm deep), Ø26 (9 mm deep) and Ø28 (2 mm deep). A small Ø4.5 hole is drilled through the dome on an incline, axis direction ≈ (−0.29, −0.66, 0.70).
- **Lower plate large stepped bore (axis Y, at X≈85.65, Z≈64.39):** Ø60 through (34 mm), with a Ø65 (9 mm) and Ø68.5 (2 mm) counterbore on the +Y (back) face and a Ø29 (11.3 mm) recess on the −Y (front) face. This is the dominant opening seen in the front and back views.
- **Lower plate small through‑holes (axis Y):** two Ø8.5 holes at (X=133.25, Z=37.69) and (X=52.75, Z=62.89); the left one (X=52.75) is provided with a narrow slot/keyway. A Ø11 through‑hole at (X=80.15, Z=11.24) near the bottom edge, counterbored Ø13 (4 mm deep).
- **Back counterbored hole (axis Y):** a Ø9 through‑hole (9 mm) at (X=104.55, Z=92.09) with a Ø13 (4 mm) counterbore, located on the −Y face to the right of the large bore.
- **Side ribs/gussets:** two curved webs (one each side) sweeping from the flange down to the lower body, visible as the arched ribs in the front and back views.

## Edges and finish
- Extensive blending: concave fillets of R1, R2, R2.5, R5, R7, R9, R15, R18 and R25 at rib‑to‑body and pocket junctions; convex rounds of R0.5, R1, R2, R3, R4, R6, R6.5, R7, R9, R10, R12, R15, R15.75, R16.75, R20 and R22 on lug tips, boss edges and the dome.
- Chamfers/tapers: 45° conical chamfers on a few bore mouths, plus small 2°–8° conical tapers at hole entries.
- Remaining edges (pocket rim, plate outline) are sharp.
