# Ribbed crankshaft pulley

**Type / function:** A flat, ring-shaped pulley disc with a ribbed outer band, mounted coaxially at the end of the crankshaft.

**Envelope:** 166.06 x 166.06 x 45.59 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 490.9 cm^3; 3 through-passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a flat annular disc whose axis of revolution is the Z axis; every feature is coaxial about the Z axis through the bounding-box centre (83.03, 83.03). It is built up from three coaxial bodies stacked along Z: a thin top flange of OD 166.06, a ribbed cylindrical band of height 30 below it, and a solid cylindrical boss of diameter 111 and length 15 at the bottom. A central bore of diameter 80 runs through the full height of the part. The body is rotationally symmetric apart from the 62-rib pattern on the band, and the model contains three separate coaxial solids (flange, band, boss).

## Features
- Central bore: 1, diameter 80, axis Z, through (45 mm long), centred at (83.03, 83.03); opens on the top flange face and the bottom boss face.
- Top flange: thin annular plate, OD 166.06, top face at Z = 45.59; it overhangs the ribbed band by 2.4 mm radially ((166.06 − 161.26)/2).
- Ribbed band: cylindrical surface of diameter 161.26 spanning Z = 15 to Z = 45 (height 30); 62 thin radial ribs, evenly spaced around the circumference, project outward from this surface. Each rib has a concave root round of diameter 6 (R3) and its two side edges are rounded R0.75.
- Bottom boss: 1, diameter 111, axis Z, length 15 (Z = 0 to 15), flat bottom face at Z = 0, coaxial with the bore.
- Because the flange, band and boss are three separate coaxial ring solids, the annular gaps between the stacked rings form two further through-passages in addition to the central bore (3 through-passages total).

## Edges and finish
- Rib side edges: convex rounds R0.75 (124 faces).
- Rib roots: concave rounds R3 (diameter 6).
- Chamfers (conical faces) at the flange outer edges and at the bore mouths, in two angles: 15° and 75° half-angle.
- All remaining edges are sharp.
