# Engine cylinder head (valve‑cover type block)

**Type / function:** A long, hollow, box‑like cover with rounded ends, carrying three aligned circular openings on one face and a row of bores with side tabs on the opposite face.

**Envelope:** 508 × 85.73 × 165.1 mm (X × Y × Z); volume 4246.4 cm³; 15 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The body is a single hollow block (a thin‑walled cover) 508 mm long in X, 165.1 mm tall in Z and 85.73 mm thick in Y. The two X‑ends are rounded in the X–Z plane with a 25.4 mm radius (four corner rounds). The large flat faces are the front face (Y‑min, seen in the front view) and the back face (Y‑max, seen in the back view); the part is symmetric about its mid‑height plane Z = 82.55 mm for the main circular features. Three identical circular feature groups are repeated along X at 146.05 mm spacing (centres X = 120.35, 266.4, 412.45 mm), and four identical side tabs are repeated at the same 146.05 mm spacing (centres X = 47.6, 193.65, 339.7 and 485.75 mm).

## Features
- **Three large circular openings on the back face:** d = 127.0 mm, axis Y, shallow (2.54 mm) through the back wall, centres at Z = 82.55 mm, X = 120.35 / 266.4 / 412.45 mm (146.05 mm pitch).
- **Six medium bores, paired in threes:** d = 28.0 mm, axis Y, depth 7.62 mm, at Z = 82.55 mm; X pairs (89.87 & 150.83), (235.92 & 296.88), (381.97 & 442.93) mm, i.e. 60.96 mm apart within each pair and centred on the three large openings. On the front face they form a single row of six circles at mid‑height.
- **Six coaxial smaller bores:** d = 6.35 mm, axis Y, depth ≈ 18.7–19.0 mm, at the same six X positions and Z = 82.55 mm (concentric with the d = 28 bores).
- **Three small angled bores on the back face:** d = 12.7 mm, length 10.7 mm, axis tilted in the Y–Z plane (toward +Z), at Z = 43.04 mm, X = 120.65 / 266.7 / 412.75 mm (one below each large opening).
- **Six small holes on the front face:** d = 5.11 mm, axis Y, depth 16.51 mm, at Z = 102.87 mm, X = 89.87 / 150.83 / 235.92 / 296.88 / 381.97 / 442.93 mm (one directly above each medium bore).
- **Four side tabs with two holes each:** small rectangular tabs projecting from the side face, each carrying two d = 5.11 mm holes (axis Y, depth 16.51 mm) at Z = 149.23 mm and Z = 111.13 mm; tab centres X = 47.6 / 193.65 / 339.7 / 485.75 mm (146.05 mm pitch). Each tab's outer edge is cut with a semicircular notch (axis X).
- **Three shallow rounded‑rectangular pockets on the top face:** aligned with the three large back openings (X = 120.35 / 266.4 / 412.45 mm), each with a small central hole.
- **One long transverse bore:** d = 25.4 mm, axis along X, length 457.15 mm, at Z = 130.18 mm, with its axis in the front‑face plane (Y = 0).

## Edges and finish
- 2.54 mm fillets (rounds) at the edges of the large circular openings and bores.
- Countersinks (≈59° half‑angle cones) on the 14 small d = 5.11 mm holes.
- Chamfers (≈5° half‑angle cones) on the six d = 28.0 mm bores.
- Remaining edges sharp.
