# Turbocharger compressor volute shell

**Type / function:** A thin‑walled compressor volute (spiral) housing for a turbocharger, providing a tangential air‑inlet pipe, a spiral flow channel that tapers to a central eye, and a central shaft/eye bore on the wheel axis.

**Envelope:** 82.14 x 156.46 x 175.01 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 146.84 cm^3; 2 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a flat snail‑shell (logarithmic‑spiral) body whose axis of wrap is the **X axis**, i.e. the line Y = 82.05, Z = 63.59. The spiral channel lies in the Y–Z plane and winds around the X axis through more than one full turn, its cross‑section tapering from a wide tangential section at the top of the body down to a small central eye on the axis. The body is a thin‑walled shell: the **‑X face is open** to the spiral channel (the wheel side), while the **+X face is closed** by a flat back plate. A straight cylindrical inlet pipe leaves the outer wrap tangentially at the top, inclined toward +Z. The form is asymmetric (the spiral breaks symmetry); the only symmetry is the cylindrical features about the X axis. The two end bosses on the X axis appear, in the front and top views, as the two rectangular tabs at mid‑height (Z ≈ 63.59) on opposite X sides.

## Features
- **Spiral volute channel:** a single continuous spiral passage in the Y–Z plane wrapping the X axis (axis line Y = 82.05, Z = 63.59), bounded by an outer wrap and an inner wall; the channel is open on the ‑X face and closed by the back plate on the +X face. The channel cross‑section tapers from the pipe end to the eye. (Profile is a smooth BSPLINE spiral; model it from the axial views.)
- **Tangential inlet pipe:** straight cylinder, outer Ø63.5, inner bore Ø60.5 (wall 1.5 mm), cylindrical length 33.27 mm; axis direction (0.00, 0.98, 0.21) — i.e. nearly +Y, inclined toward +Z — with the axis midpoint at (41.07, 133.58, 140.5). The pipe is tangent to the outer wrap at the top of the body, blends smoothly (tangentially) into the spiral, and its mouth is open (the inlet).
- **Central hub / eye:** cylinder Ø20 on the X axis from X = 10 to X = 72.14 (length 62.14), coaxial with the volute, forming the eye of the spiral.
- **Shaft bore:** Ø17 along the X axis, length 72.14, from X = 10 to X = 82.14, centre line (Y = 82.05, Z = 63.59); passes through the hub and the +X end boss and is open at the +X face (and at the volute interior at the ‑X end). **Through.**
- **+X end boss:** cylinder Ø30, length 10, on the X axis at X = 72.14–82.14 (centre 77.14), projecting from the +X back plate and carrying the shaft bore.
- **‑X end rim:** cylinder Ø44.88, length 10, on the X axis at X = 0–10 (centre 5.0), at the open (‑X) end, forming the outer edge of the spiral's leading wall.
- **Small radial tab:** a thin flat tab in the central eye, radial in the Y–Z plane, joining the hub to the eye wall; visible on both axial faces (no separate dimension).
- The two X‑axis bosses above are the same features seen as the left (taller, Ø44.88, at ‑X) and right (shorter, Ø30, at +X) tabs in the front view.

The two through‑passages are: (1) the flow path open pipe mouth → Ø60.5 pipe bore → spiral channel → central eye, and (2) the Ø17 shaft bore along X.

## Edges and finish
- No fillets, rounds, or chamfers (no tori, cones, or partial‑cylinder arcs); all edges are sharp, including the pipe mouth, the boss face edges, and the spiral channel edges.
- The pipe‑to‑body junction is a smooth tangent blend (continuous BSPLINE), not a discrete fillet.
