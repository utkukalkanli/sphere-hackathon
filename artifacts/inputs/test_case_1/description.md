# Camshaft retainer bracket

**Type / function:** A small retaining bracket – a flat plate carrying two bolt holes and a large cylindrical clearance recess, with a flat locating rib on the rear face.

**Envelope:** 19.0 x 19.0 x 50.8 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 9.20 cm^3; 2 through-passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a flat rectangular plate standing vertically, its long axis along Z (50.8 mm), 19.0 mm wide in X and 14.0 mm thick in Y (front face at Y = 0, rear face at Y = 14). A large cylindrical recess is milled into the front face across the middle of the plate, and a flat rib projects from the rear face across the same middle band, so that the section seen from the side reads as a "C" (recess on the front side, rib on the rear side). The part is symmetric about the vertical plane X = 9.5 and about the horizontal plane at mid‑height Z = 25.4; it is **not** symmetric front‑to‑back. The 14.0 mm plate thickness is the length of the through‑holes; the 5.0 mm difference to the 19.0 mm bounding‑box depth is the rear rib projection.

## Features
- **Main plate:** rectangular block 19.0 (X) x 50.8 (Z) x 14.0 (Y) mm; flat front face (Y = 0) and flat rear face (Y = 14).
- **Mounting holes:** 2, diameter 6.75 mm, axis along Y, **through** the 14.0 mm plate (the only through‑passages). Centres at X = 9.5, Z = 6.35 and X = 9.5, Z = 44.45 – i.e. centred in X, 38.1 mm apart, and 6.35 mm in from each Z end.
- **Front recess:** 1 large concave cylindrical pocket, diameter 25.4 mm, axis parallel to X, centred at mid‑height (Z = 25.4 mm) and on the plate mid‑thickness (Y = 7.0 mm). It is **blind** (does not break through the rear face). Because its 25.4 mm diameter exceeds the 19.0 mm width, the recess spans the full width in X; its opening on the front face is therefore bounded by two horizontal lines, and its true circular profile is seen when looking along X.
- **Rear rib:** 1 flat rib on the rear face, protruding 5.0 mm in +Y (from Y = 14 out to Y = 19), spanning the full 19.0 mm width in X over a central band in Z. Its top and bottom edges are two concave cylindrical surfaces of diameter 25.0 mm with axes parallel to Y, centred in X; these two surfaces appear as the two arcs on the rear face.
