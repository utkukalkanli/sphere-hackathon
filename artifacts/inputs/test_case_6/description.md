# Conical sunburst‑finned air‑filter element (intake cone)

**Type / function:** A solid, axisymmetric conical element carrying a radial sunburst of triangular fins, a domed top cap and a flanged base, designated as an engine air filter / intake cone.

**Envelope:** 140 x 140 x 136.5 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 377.9 cm^3; 0 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a single solid built around the Z axis, like a truncated cone (frustum) that is wider at the bottom and narrower at the top, capped at each end by a rounded disc. The lower end is a 140 mm‑diameter flange; the upper end is a 73 mm‑diameter domed cap; between them the body tapers conically from the flange to the cap. The conical surface is not smooth but is faceted into a circumferential "sunburst" of triangular fins separated by V‑shaped grooves that run the full height of the cone. The overall envelope is a cone of revolution, so the part is symmetric about the Z axis in its outer profile; the fin pattern is four‑fold symmetric about Z, with one fin centred on each of the +X, −X, +Y and −Y directions and the remaining fins equally spaced between them. The bottom of the part (z = 0) is the flat face of the flange; the top (z = 136.5) is the flat face of the cap.

## Features
- **Bottom flange:** Ø140 mm disc, overall thickness 27.5 mm (z = 0 to 27.5). It has a 7.5 mm‑tall cylindrical side band of Ø140 mm centred at z = 13.75, with an R10 round on both its top and bottom outer edges. The flat bottom face is an annulus from the central bore (Ø30) out to Ø120 (the inner edge of the bottom R10 round).
- **Top cap:** Ø73 mm dome, overall thickness 27.5 mm (z = 109 to 136.5). It has a 7.5 mm‑tall cylindrical side band of Ø73 mm centred at z = 122.75, with an R10 round on both its top and bottom outer edges. The flat top face is Ø53 (the inner edge of the top R10 round).
- **Conical body:** tapers along Z from a Ø120 mm base at the flange top (z = 27.5) to a Ø53 mm top at the cap base (z = 109), i.e. the flange and cap diameters less the two R10 edge rounds; height ≈ 81.5 mm. The cone meets the flange and the cap at the inner edges of their R10 rounds (rounded shoulders at both junctions).
- **Sunburst fins / grooves:** a radial array of triangular fins on the conical surface, four‑fold symmetric with a fin on each cardinal axis (±X, ±Y) and the rest equally spaced around the circumference. Each groove is a V cut radially into the cone, bounded by two flat (planar) walls meeting at a sharp crease at the groove bottom; the raised fins between grooves are gently curved conical patches. The grooves run continuously from the flange top to the cap base.
- **Central blind bore:** one Ø30 mm cylindrical bore, axis Z, on the bottom face, centred at (70, 70). Depth 17.5 mm (z = 0 to 17.5); it is blind and does not break through the top of the flange.

## Edges and finish
- R10 rounds (four in total) on the four outer circular edges: both edges of the bottom flange and both edges of the top cap.
- No chamfers or countersinks anywhere.
- All remaining edges are sharp, including the V‑groove creases, the fin tips, the bore mouth, and the blind bore bottom.
