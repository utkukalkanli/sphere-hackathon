# Engine valve cover

**Type / function:** A thin, elongated cover plate (valve cover) with notched long edges, a pattern of small blind holes, and a longitudinal rib/bore.

**Envelope:** 63.5 x 165.1 x 508.0 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 1046.5 cm^3; 8 through-passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a flat, slab-like cover whose long axis is Z (508.0 mm), its width is Y (165.1 mm) and its thickness is X (63.5 mm). The two large flat faces are normal to X; the X=0 face carries the small-hole pattern and the X=63.5 face carries a longitudinal rib. The outline in the Y–Z plane is a long rectangle with rounded ends, interrupted by open C-shaped notches along both long edges and small relief notches at the four corners. The two long edges are not equivalent: the Y=0 edge carries three large notches plus two smaller ones, while the Y=165.1 edge carries only smaller notches, so the part has no mirror symmetry about the Y mid-plane. A single longitudinal rib and a parallel longitudinal bore run along the back face near the Y=0 edge for most of the Z length.

## Features
- Eight small blind holes, diameter 6.76 mm, axis X, depth 7.62 mm, opening on the X=0 face (centres at X=3.81). Each is seated in a shallow C-shaped recess (recess rim ≈ 19.46 mm) that opens toward the nearest edge or corner. Positions in (Y, Z): four corner holes at (10.16, 10.16), (154.94, 10.16), (10.16, 497.84), (154.94, 497.84); four mid-edge holes at (154.94, 390.43), (154.94, 96.53), (10.16, 319.23), (10.16, 158.29).
- Three large open C-shaped notches cut through the X thickness on the Y=0 long edge, alternating with two smaller C-shaped notches on the same edge (the smaller ones align with the two Y=10.16 mid-edge holes).
- Two smaller open C-shaped notches on the Y=165.1 long edge, aligned with the two Y=154.94 mid-edge holes; the four corners each carry a small relief notch around the corner hole.
- One longitudinal convex rib/boss, diameter 10.0 mm, axis Z, length 441.8 mm, centred at (X=58.5, Y=5.0), running along the X=63.5 face near the Y=0 edge (not through; length < 508).
- One longitudinal bore, diameter 6.0 mm, axis Z, length 442.57 mm, centred at (X=54.15, Y=9.35), parallel to and just inboard of the rib (not through).

## Edges and finish
- Convex rounds: R25.4 (4 faces, large notch/corner transitions), R7.5 (6 faces), R5.0 (8 faces, recess rims).
- Concave fillets: R19.05 (4 faces), R3.0 (8 faces), R1.15 (6 faces).
- Torus fillets of minor radius 3.0 (21 faces) and 5.0 (21 faces) at the notch and recess edges.
- 42 conical chamfer faces at 5° half-angle at hole and notch edges.
- Remaining edges sharp.
