# Camshaft bushing (straight cylindrical sleeve)

**Type / function:** A simple straight cylindrical sleeve/bushing that provides a precision bore for a rotating shaft (camshaft bearing liner in the engine assembly).

**Envelope:** 19.05 x 25.4 x 25.4 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 4.22 cm^3; 1 through-passage.

## Overall shape
The part is a hollow, straight tube of constant wall thickness, i.e. a revolved body formed by subtracting a coaxial inner bore from an outer cylinder. Its cylindrical axis is parallel to X, and the body is fully symmetric about that axis and about the mid-plane at X = 9.525 mm. The only flat surfaces are the two annular end faces, both perpendicular to X. There are no ribs, flanges, lugs, pockets or external features; the silhouette in the front, back, top and bottom directions is a plain rectangle, and the left/right directions show a uniform ring.

## Features
- Outer cylindrical surface: one convex cylinder, Ø 25.4 mm, axis along X, length 19.05 mm, axis centred at Y = 12.7 mm, Z = 12.7 mm (spans the full X extent, X = 0 to 19.05 mm).
- Inner bore: one concave cylinder, Ø 19.05 mm, axis along X, coaxial with the outer surface, running the full 19.05 mm length as a through-hole (the single through-passage).
- End faces: two flat annular faces perpendicular to X, located at X = 0 and X = 19.05 mm, each bounded by the Ø 19.05 mm bore and the Ø 25.4 mm outer diameter.
- Wall thickness: 3.175 mm all around (derived from (25.4 − 19.05)/2), uniform over the whole length.
- No additional holes, counterbores, bosses, slots or pockets of any kind.

## Edges and finish
- No fillets, rounds or chamfers on any edge.
- All edges are sharp: the four circular edges where the bore meets the outer diameter at the two ends, plus the corresponding circular edges on the outer surface.
