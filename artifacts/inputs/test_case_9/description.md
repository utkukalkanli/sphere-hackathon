# V6 engine block (six‑cylinder V‑block)

**Type / function:** A six‑cylinder V‑engine block: two inclined cylinder banks at 90° to each other, each carrying three bores, with a transverse semicircular slot for the crankshaft.

**Envelope:** 508.0 x 255.94 x 511.87 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 14768 cm^3; 6 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a 508 mm long block whose length runs along X. Its Y–Z cross‑section is an X/cross profile: two thick inclined banks (the cylinder banks) meet at a +Y apex to form a 90° V opening toward +Y, while the –Y side is a flat base face. Each bank's outer inclined face carries an integral flat head plate (the head/gasket face) set in a rounded recessed frame; the three bore openings and the bolt holes are cut in this plate. The two banks are exact mirror images of one another across the mid‑Z plane (Z = 255.94), so the part is symmetric top‑to‑bottom in Z but **not** symmetric in X (the two banks are offset in X). A semicircular slot is cut into the –Y base face at mid‑height, running the full X length.

## Features
- **Cylinder bores – 6 × Ø127.0**, axis at 45° to Y and Z (in the Y–Z plane), cylindrical length 156.14 mm, **through** (open on the inclined head face and through to the opposite face). Two banks of three:
  - Upper bank, axis direction (0, +0.71, +0.71): centres X = 95.25 / 241.3 / 387.35, all at Y = 142.36, Z = 398.3; X‑spacing 146.05 mm.
  - Lower bank, axis direction (0, +0.71, −0.71): centres X = 120.65 / 266.7 / 412.75, all at Y = 142.36, Z = 113.58; X‑spacing 146.05 mm (bank offset +25.4 mm in X relative to the upper bank).
- **Inclined head plates – 2** (one per bank): flat rounded‑rectangle plates on the inclined faces, each with 3 large Ø127 openings coaxial with the bores and 8 small holes, rounded outer corners, seated in a rounded recessed frame.
- **Bolt holes – 16 × Ø9.52**, axis parallel to the bank bore axes (45° in Y–Z), **blind**, depth 76.0 mm. 8 per bank in a 4 (in X) × 2 (in the inclined plane, inner/outer) grid, placed at the four diagonal corners of each Ø127 bore (corner holes shared between adjacent bores):
  - Upper bank: X = 22.61 / 167.89 / 314.71 / 459.99, at (Y,Z) = (210.21, 387.12) and (131.18, 466.15).
  - Lower bank: X = 48.01 / 193.29 / 340.11 / 485.39, at (Y,Z) = (210.21, 124.75) and (131.18, 45.73).
- **Transverse crankshaft slot – 1 × Ø127.0** (R63.5) semicircular slot on the –Y base face at mid‑height Z = 255.94, axis along X, running the full 508 mm and open at both X faces.

## Edges and finish
- Outer corners of the head plates and the block outline are rounded with convex rounds (R25.4), with larger convex rounds (R50.8 and R76.2) on the V‑apex and slot‑region edges.
- Concave fillets R6.35 and R12.7 at the head‑plate/frame junctions and around the bore and bolt‑hole edges; concave fillet R50.8 where the slot meets the base face.
- No chamfers (no conical faces); remaining edges are sharp.
