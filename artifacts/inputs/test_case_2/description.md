# Engine poppet valve (head and stem)

**Type / function:** A revolved poppet‑type engine valve – a thin circular head (valve disc) carrying a long, thin concentric stem – used as a gas‑control valve in the engine.

**Envelope:** 53.34 x 85.55 x 53.34 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 10.95 cm^3; 0 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a body of revolution about the Y axis, so it is fully axisymmetric (the X and Z extents are equal and the head is centred in both). It is built up from two coaxial revolved solids: a thin circular head lying in the X–Z plane at the −Y end, and a long, slender cylindrical stem that is concentric with the head and projects in the +Y direction from the head's back. The head presents a flat face on the −Y side and a smooth concave dish on the +Y side; the stem emerges from the centre of that dish and runs the full length of the part. There are no holes, bores or slots – the head and stem are both solid, so the part has no through‑passage.

## Features
- **Valve head (disc):** solid circular disc, outer diameter 53.34, cylindrical rim thickness 1.27, axis along Y, centred at X = 26.67, Z = 26.67 (i.e. mid‑X and mid‑Z of the bounding box). The −Y face is a flat circular face; the +Y face is a concave dish that flares from the stem out to the rim.
- **Stem:** solid cylinder, diameter 6.36, straight cylindrical length 66.35, axis along Y, concentric with the head (X = 26.67, Z = 26.67). It projects from the concave back in +Y; the free tip is at the +Y extreme of the part, giving an overall head‑to‑tip length of 85.55. The stem is solid (no bore).
- **Root / dish fillets:** two R15.25 blends on the +Y (concave) side – one joining the stem root to the dish and one running the dish out to the head rim – producing the smooth concave profile seen from the back.
- **Chamfers:** 45° chamfers on the head rim (both the −Y and +Y rim edges) and on the free tip of the stem.

## Edges and finish
- 45° chamfers on the head rim (front and back edges) and on the stem tip.
- R15.25 fillets at the stem root and at the dish‑to‑rim junction on the concave back.
- All remaining edges are smooth revolved tangencies (head rim to faces, stem to fillets); no additional rounds or breaks.
