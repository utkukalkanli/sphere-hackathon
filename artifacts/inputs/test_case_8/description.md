# Hydraulic pump main body

**Type / function:** A machined block‑type pump housing with a central figure‑eight through window, a transverse main bore, a bolted flange and two mounting feet.

**Envelope:** 80 x 128 x 40.2 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 121.46 cm^3; 11 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a single rectangular block 80 mm wide (X), 128 mm long (Y) and 40.2 mm high (Z), built up as a solid web with a large opening in the middle and two integral mounting feet at the –Y end. The long axis of the body is Y; the top and bottom faces (Z = 0 and Z = 40.2) are the large flanged faces, the front and back faces (Y = 0 and Y = 128) are the second‑largest faces, and the left and right faces (X = 0 and X = 80) are the narrow end faces. The body is mirror‑symmetric about the central Y–Z plane (X = 40). A vertical figure‑eight window runs straight through the centre in Z, a 20 mm bore runs through the centre in X, and the feet project to the –Y end, so the part is not symmetric in Y or Z.

## Features
- **Central through window:** a vertical figure‑eight opening formed by two overlapping circular bores of 44.45 mm diameter (axes along Z), passing completely through the 40.2 mm height and centred on the X = 40 and Y = 72.95 mid‑planes.
- **Flange bolt holes:** 8 holes, 7.0 mm diameter, axes along Z, through the full 40.2 mm height, with chamfered openings. They sit on the flange around the window at centres (X, Y): (40.0, 122.0) and (40.0, 23.9) on the centreline; (18.79, 113.21), (61.21, 113.21), (18.79, 32.69), (61.21, 32.69) forming a 42.42 × 80.52 mm rectangle; and (16.0, 72.95), (64.0, 72.95) on the horizontal centreline. The two lower corner holes (18.79, 32.69) and (61.21, 32.69) are carried in raised 12.0 mm diameter full‑height bosses.
- **Main transverse bore:** 1 bore, 20.0 mm diameter, axis along X, through the central web (66 mm long), centred at (40.0, 72.95, 20.1).
- **Cross holes:** 4 holes, 4.2 mm diameter, axes along X, through the central web (66 mm long), arranged on a 25 × 25 mm square about the main‑bore axis at centres (40.0, 85.45, 32.6), (40.0, 85.45, 7.6), (40.0, 60.45, 7.6), (40.0, 60.45, 32.6).
- **Front‑face blind holes:** 4 holes, 6.0 mm diameter, axes along Y, blind to a depth of 4 mm from the Y = 0 face, at centres (4.62, 2.0, 9.0), (4.62, 2.0, 31.2), (75.38, 2.0, 31.2), (75.38, 2.0, 9.0).
- **Semicircular edge features:** 12.0 mm diameter half‑cylinders with axes along Y, open to the top and bottom faces so they read as half‑circles on the end faces. The back face (Y = 128) carries three concave recesses along the top edge and three matching convex bosses along the bottom edge; the front face (Y = 0) carries one concave recess (top) and one convex boss (bottom) on the centreline.
- **Mounting feet:** two feet at the –Y end, one on each side of the centreline, each with a 45° chamfered outer corner, a concave curved relief on the inner side, a rectangular blind pocket with rounded corners on the outer face, and a shallow stepped groove on the top face.

## Edges and finish
- Chamfers (conical faces at 59° half‑angle) dress the openings of the through holes, the main bore and the cross holes.
- Concave fillets of R2 at the internal junctions of the central window and flange; larger concave fillets of R12 and R18 at the waist of the figure‑eight window and at the foot‑to‑body transitions.
- Small R1 convex rounds and R1 toroidal rounds on the flange lobe edges and the foot edges; remaining edges sharp.
