# Crankshaft bushing (flanged sleeve)

**Type / function:** A cylindrical sleeve-type bushing with a constant through-bore and a thin flanged collar at one axial end, providing a round bearing seat.

**Envelope:** 21.59 x 132.08 x 132.08 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 177.66 cm^3; 1 through-passage.

## Overall shape
The part is a short, wide, rotationally symmetric revolved body (a sleeve) whose axis runs along X. It is built as a tube of constant inner diameter with a stepped outer diameter: a longer main sleeve over most of the length, and a thin flange/collar of larger diameter at the +X end. The whole body is cylindrically symmetric about the X axis; no feature breaks that symmetry. The cross-section in the Y–Z plane is an annulus centred at Y = 66.04, Z = 66.04.

## Features
- **Central bore:** Ø76.2, axis along X, running all the way through the part (length 21.59 mm); this is the only through-passage. Bore axis is centred in the Y–Z section (Y = 66.04, Z = 66.04).
- **Main sleeve outer surface:** Ø127.0, coaxial with the bore, length 19.05 mm, occupying X = 0 to X = 19.05. Radial wall thickness here is 25.4 mm.
- **End flange / collar outer surface:** Ø132.08, coaxial, thickness 2.54 mm, at the +X end (X = 19.05 to X = 21.59). It protrudes 2.54 mm radially beyond the main sleeve OD; the flange is a solid ring of radial thickness 27.94 mm (its inner surface is the continuous Ø76.2 bore, so the bore passes straight through the flange).
- **End / step faces (all perpendicular to X):** at X = 0 an annular face from Ø76.2 to Ø127.0; at X = 19.05 a step annular face from Ø127.0 to Ø132.08; at X = 21.59 an annular face from Ø76.2 to Ø132.08.

## Edges and finish
- No fillets, rounds, or chamfers anywhere; all edges are sharp.
- Outer and inner surfaces are plain full cylinders (no grooves, flats, or ribs).
