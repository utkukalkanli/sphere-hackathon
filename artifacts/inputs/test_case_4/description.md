# Three‑throw crankshaft

**Type / function:** A three‑throw crankshaft – a stepped rotating shaft that carries three offset crank journals (pins) joined to the central axis by flat counterweight webs.

**Envelope:** 211.59 x 196.85 x 564.88 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 3472.0 cm^3; 0 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a single solid whose long axis runs along Z (overall length 564.88 mm). It is built up as a stepped central shaft (the rotation axis) with three crank throws spaced along its length; each throw consists of an offset cylindrical crank pin tied back to the central shaft by a flat, radially‑oriented counterweight web (plate). The three throws are placed at three different axial stations but at 120° angular positions about the axis, which is why the plan (top/bottom) silhouette is a three‑lobed "trefoil" with a circular hub in the middle. The two free ends of the shaft are plain, smaller‑diameter journals (the snouts). Because the three throws sit at different Z positions, the part has no plane or rotational symmetry; the only regularity is the 120° angular spacing of the throw directions. The rotation axis lies at the X‑centre of the envelope and is offset in Y (the throws are not symmetric about Y).

## Features
- **Central stepped shaft (axis Z):** a smaller‑diameter journal of Ø76.2 at each free end (top and bottom, each 92.71 mm long), stepped up to a Ø88.9 main‑journal section 379.46 mm long that spans the whole throw region (Z 92.71–472.17 from the bottom face).
- **Crank pins (throws):** 3 convex cylinders, Ø101.6, axial length 87.54 mm, each offset 63.5 mm from the rotation axis. Axial centres at Z = 136.48, 282.44 and 428.4 (equal 145.96 mm spacing; the lowest pin starts where the Ø88.9 section begins and the top pin ends where it finishes). The three offset directions are 120° apart: the lowest throw points toward −Y, the middle throw toward −X/+Y, and the top throw toward +X/+Y (the latter two symmetric about +Y).
- **Counterweight webs:** 3 flat plates, one per throw, each lying in the radial plane of its throw and joining the crank pin to the central shaft. Each web is 54.62 mm thick in Z, has a rounded outer end of Ø76.2, and is axially centred on its crank pin (Z‑centres 136.48 / 282.44 / 428.4).
- **Holes/bores:** none – the part is a solid with no through‑ or blind‑holes.

## Edges and finish
- 2.54 mm convex rounds (24 faces) breaking the sharp edges of the webs and the journal/pin diameter steps.
- 169.0 mm convex round (12 faces) – a broad convex blend on the counterweight webs.
- No chamfers or countersinks; no through‑passages.
