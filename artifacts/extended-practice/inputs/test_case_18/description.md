# Rocking-arm coil spring

**Type / function:** A small helical coil spring with flat circular end plates, used as a rocking-arm spring in the engine valvetrain.

**Envelope:** 27.25 x 30.3 x 27.25 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 3.483 cm^3; 1 through-passage.

## Overall shape
The part is a single continuous round wire wound into a helix whose axis is the Y axis, with a flat circular plate (washer) joined to the wire at each end; the whole body is coaxial about the Y axis. It is built up as an axisymmetric stack along Y: a helical coil in the middle, capped by a thin disc at the minimum-Y end and a solid disc at the maximum-Y end. The form is fully axisymmetric about the Y axis (circular in both X and Z), but it is not symmetric about the mid-Y plane because the two end plates differ (one carries a central hole, the other is solid). The coil's outer diameter (26.7 mm) lies just inside the end-plate outer diameter (27.25 mm), so the plates project slightly beyond the coil. The overall length along Y is 30.3 mm.

## Features
- Helical coil: one round wire of uniform circular cross-section wound into a multi-turn helix, axis along Y, outer diameter 26.7 mm, spanning the full 30.3 mm length between the end plates; the turns are evenly spaced and the wire ends are rounded where they meet the plates.
- End plates: two flat circular discs of outer diameter 27.25 mm, coaxial with the coil axis (Y), one at each end of the helix. The plate on the minimum-Y face is 1.5 mm thick; the plate on the maximum-Y face is a flat solid disc.
- Central hole: one circular hole of diameter 12.7 mm, axis along Y, in the minimum-Y end plate only, concentric with the part axis (centre at X = 13.63, Z = 13.63); it passes through the 1.5 mm plate (hole length 1.5 mm). The maximum-Y end plate has no hole.

## Edges and finish
- The coil wire has a smooth round cross-section with rounded turn transitions; no chamfers or fillets are present on the wire.
- The end plates have sharp circular edges on their outer and inner rims, and the central hole edge is sharp.
