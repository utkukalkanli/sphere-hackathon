# Engine side cover plate

**Type / function:** A thin flat cover plate that encloses the side of the engine, providing two aligned through‑openings in raised bosses and a large semicircular relief at one edge.

**Envelope:** 477.76 x 318.9 x 52.15 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 629.1 cm^3; 2 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a thin, flat cover plate whose main faces are normal to Z and whose overall thickness is 52.15 mm. The plan outline (in X–Y) is an irregular, crown‑like shape that is mirror‑symmetric about the mid‑plane perpendicular to X (X = 238.88 mm from the X‑min corner): a V‑shaped notch is cut into the +Y edge, a large semicircular opening is cut into the −Y edge, and the remaining corners terminate in sharp points with one concave circular scallop on each outer side (left and right). The side walls are inclined (drafted), so the body reads as a shallow trapezoid in the X–Z and Y–Z directions and the plan outline of the two faces differs slightly. The top face carries the raised bosses, ribs and circular pads; the bottom face carries matching raised rings and a curved rib. The two through‑holes are the only passages that go all the way through the part.

## Features
- **Body / plate:** flat plate, main faces normal to Z, thickness 52.15 mm; inclined side walls give a trapezoidal side profile; outline mirror‑symmetric about the mid‑X plane.
- **Two through‑holes:** axis in Z, placed symmetrically about the mid‑X plane in the upper (+Y) half of the plate, roughly midway between the V‑notch and the semicircular cutout; each hole is seated in a raised circular boss with a flat annular top, surrounded by a shallow concentric circular pad, all blended to the plate by fillets; these two holes are the only through‑passages.
- **Semicircular cutout:** large through relief, axis in Z, centered on the mid‑X plane and opening to the −Y edge.
- **V‑notch:** cut into the +Y edge, centered on the mid‑X plane, with two slanted sides meeting a short flat base; a low raised rib/ledge sits at the base of this notch, projecting slightly proud of the top face.
- **Curved rib:** a flat‑topped raised band on the top face that arcs between the two boss regions over the semicircular cutout; a matching curved rib is present on the bottom face.
- **Perimeter scallops and points:** one concave circular scallop on each outer side edge (left and right), and six sharp pointed corners on the outline (two top‑outer, two side‑outer, and two lower corners flanking the semicircular cutout).

## Edges and finish
- Fillets and rounds of radii 0.25, 2.54, 3.65, 6.35, 10, 12.7, 19.05, 25.4 and 31.75 mm are used throughout — the larger values form the scallop and cutout transitions, the mid values (12.7, 19.05) blend the bosses, pads and ribs, and the small values (0.25–6.35) act as edge breaks.
- A few 5° chamfers (conical faces) break otherwise sharp edges, including at the through‑hole openings.
- The six pointed outline corners are left sharp (no round).
