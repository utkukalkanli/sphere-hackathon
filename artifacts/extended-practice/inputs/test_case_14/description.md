# Engine intake manifold (looped tubular body with central mounting bracket)

**Type / function:** A single‑piece looped tubular manifold (engine intake manifold) made of one continuous round tube that forms a closed oval ring, combined with a central mounting bracket (two parallel flange plates, a rib lattice and a central block) that bolts the loop to the engine.

**Envelope:** 994.87 x 280.78 x 410.08 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 7308.1 cm^3; 9 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is one solid built from two joined systems: (1) a long, closed, constant‑cross‑section round tube that forms a single oval ring, and (2) a compact central bracket that the ring passes through. The ring is strongly elongated along X (it spans almost the full 994.87 mm X‑extent) and is narrow in Y, so it reads as a wide oval when seen along Y and as a thin oval when seen along Z. The ring is asymmetric: the –X end of the ring is turned in the X–Z plane (a U‑bend seen edge‑on from the side), whereas the +X end is turned in the Y–Z plane (a circular bend seen face‑on from the side); the two long legs of the ring run roughly parallel to X and pass through the central bracket at mid‑height. The bracket sits in the middle of the X‑range and is built up as two thin horizontal rectangular plates (parallel to the X–Y plane, one upper and one lower) joined by a lattice of flat diagonal ribs, with a rectangular block at mid‑height between them. There is no mirror symmetry about the mid‑X plane because the two ring ends lie in different planes.

## Features
- **Looped runner tube:** one continuous hollow tube of constant circular cross‑section forming a single closed oval ring (one through‑passage). Long axis along X; –X bend in the X–Z plane, +X bend in the Y–Z plane; the straight leg that runs along X through the central block is a hollow cylinder, outer diameter 63.5 mm, inner bore 38.1 mm (wall 12.7 mm), with a reduced‑diameter collar of 50.8 mm at each end where it meets the block. This X‑axis coaxial system (Ø63.5 / Ø50.8 / Ø38.1) is centred at X = 541.68, Y = 106.25, Z = 184.1 from the minimum bounding‑box corner, and the Ø63.5/Ø38.1 run is 334.33 mm long (the Ø50.8 collar 314.33 mm long).
- **Upper flange plate:** one thin horizontal rectangular plate parallel to X–Y at the top of the bracket; carries 3 through‑holes in a single row along X, equally spaced (one near each end and one at mid‑length).
- **Lower flange plate:** one thin horizontal rectangular plate parallel to X–Y at the bottom of the bracket, directly below the upper plate; carries 3 through‑holes in a single row along X, equally spaced and aligned in Y with the upper‑plate holes.
- **Rib lattice:** a set of flat diagonal struts connecting the upper and lower plates, arranged as crossing diagonals (an X‑shaped web) in the X–Z plane, forming the side walls of the bracket and tying the two plates together.
- **Central block:** one rectangular box at mid‑height of the bracket, elongated along X, with rounded vertical edges; the horizontal runner leg (Ø63.5/Ø38.1) passes straight through it along X, and the Ø50.8 collars sit on its ±X faces.

## Edges and finish
- R15 convex rounds on the vertical edges of the central block.
- R5 convex rounds (small edge rounds on the bracket).
- R6.35 concave fillets at the junctions of the ribs with the plates and the block.
- Ø12.7 concave circular grooves (rounds) on the runner collars where the horizontal leg meets the block, one on each side.
- The looped tube is a smooth, tangent‑continuous swept surface with no chamfers; all other external edges are as listed above.
