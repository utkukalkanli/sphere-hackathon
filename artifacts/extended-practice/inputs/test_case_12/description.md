# Engine belt pulley (ribbed belt wheel)

**Type / function:** A small ribbed belt pulley for an engine accessory drive — an axisymmetric revolved body with 22 axial ribs between two flanges, a central shaft bore, and a short hub boss on the bottom face.

**Envelope:** 60.29 x 60.29 x 40.59 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 70.18 cm^3; 3 through-passages.

## Overall shape
- The part is a revolved body about the Z axis, 60.29 mm in overall diameter and 40.59 mm tall, with its axis at (30.15, 30.15) in X/Y.
- It is built up in four stacked elements along Z: a short cylindrical hub boss at the Z = 0 end, a thin bottom flange disc, a tall ribbed band, and a thin top flange disc.
- The ribbed band is a hollow drum: its outer surface carries 22 vertical ribs and its inner surface is a smooth bore of 55.49 mm diameter.
- The rib pattern has 22-fold rotational symmetry about Z; the part is not symmetric top-to-bottom because the hub boss exists only on the bottom (Z = 0) face.
- The flange discs overhang the rib band slightly, giving the characteristic pulley silhouette seen in the side views.

## Features
- **Central bore:** one 12.0 mm diameter hole, axis Z, centred at (30.15, 30.15), running through the part from the hub face (Z = 0) to the top flange (length 40.0 mm).
- **Hub boss:** one 43.0 mm diameter convex cylinder, axis Z, on the bottom face, from Z = 0 to Z = 10 (length 10.0 mm), concentric with the bore.
- **Band inner bore:** one 55.49 mm diameter concave cylinder, axis Z, from Z = 10 to Z = 40 (length 30.0 mm); it opens into the bottom face and is closed off at the top by the solid top flange, forming the hollow drum interior.
- **Ribs:** 22 vertical (Z-axis) ribs evenly spaced around the circumference between the flanges (360/22 ≈ 16.4° pitch), spanning the full height of the band; each rib has two rounded outer edges.
- **Groove roots:** the 22 gaps between adjacent ribs have rounded concave bottoms, each a partial cylinder of 6.0 mm diameter (R3).
- **Flanges:** two discs of 60.29 mm diameter at the top and bottom of the rib band; the bottom flange is an annulus around the 55.49 mm bore, the top flange is a solid disc carrying the 12 mm bore.
- **Chamfers:** conical faces break the flange rim edges — eight cones at 15° half-angle and twelve shallow cones at 75° half-angle.

## Edges and finish
- R0.75 convex rounds on all 44 rib outer edges.
- R3 (6.0 mm diameter) concave rounds at the 22 groove roots.
- One large convex round of R27.25 on a flange outer edge.
- Conical chamfers as listed above; remaining edges (bore mouths, hub bottom) are sharp.
