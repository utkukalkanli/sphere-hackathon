# Camshaft

**Type / function:** A camshaft – a long, slender stepped shaft carrying six cam lobes, mounted in the engine block (one of a pair) to impart valve motion.

**Envelope:** 34.7 x 38.1 x 463.09 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 145.3 cm^3; 0 through-passages.

## Overall shape
A single solid, slender revolved body oriented along Z, 463.09 mm long, with a small near-circular cross-section. The shaft axis lies at the centre of the X–Y bounding box (X = 17.35, Y = 19.05). The body is a stepped Z-axis cylinder: a larger-diameter central section with two smaller-diameter plain end sections (the step is visible as the two concentric circles in the plan outline). Six flat radial cam lobes (tabs) are added to the central section at six discrete stations; because each lobe points in a different radial direction, the part has no overall symmetry and its plan (top/bottom) silhouette is a six-petal star. The lobes, not the round body, define the overall X (34.7) and Y (38.1) extents. There are no internal cavities or through-holes.

## Features
- **Stepped shaft body (Z axis, axis at X = 17.35, Y = 19.05):** central section Ø25.4 mm, 360.68 mm long (spanning Z ≈ 50.3 to Z ≈ 411.0); two plain end sections Ø19.0 mm making up the remaining length (≈50.3 mm at the Z = 0 end and ≈52.1 mm at the Z = 463.09 end). The Ø19.0 surface runs the full 463.09 mm as the core/step line.
- **Cam lobes (6):** flat radial tabs projecting from the central Ø25.4 section, one per station, distributed along that section. Each lobe is a small flat block with a rounded nose; the rounded outer edge of each lobe is a convex partial cylinder of Ø12.7 mm. The radial (angular) orientation of the lobes is phased differently from station to station, so in front/side elevation the tabs appear to alternate left and right, and in plan they trace the six-petal star.
- **Holes/bores:** none (the part is a solid with no through or blind cylindrical holes).

## Edges and finish
- Lobe noses rounded with R6.35 mm (the Ø12.7 mm convex partial cylinders, six of them).
- All other edges (shaft/lobe intersections, the Ø25.4-to-Ø19.0 step, lobe side and end faces) are sharp; no additional fillets, rounds, or chamfers are present.
