# Engine Oil Pan

**Type / function:** A deep, rectangular oil‑collection pan (tray) with a flat bolted flange frame on one face and a closed, crowned bowl on the other, used as the sump at the base of the engine.

**Envelope:** 508.0 x 127.0 x 330.2 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 3567.8 cm³; 12 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a rectangular tray built up as a flat flange frame on the +Y face (the mating/gasket face) with a deep bowl recessed on the −Y side. The flange is a rectangular frame 508.0 mm (X) by 330.2 mm (Z) with rounded outer corners; the bowl is a large pocket inset from the flange edges, leaving a peripheral rim that carries the bolt holes, and the bowl bottom is a closed, convex crowned surface that bulges toward −Y. The long axis of the pan is X (508.0 mm); the depth of the bowl is along Y (127.0 mm total, of which the rim is only 9.4 mm thick); the height is Z (330.2 mm). The body is mirror‑symmetric about the mid‑X plane (X = 254.0 mm) and the bolt pattern is mirror‑symmetric top‑to‑bottom about the mid‑Z plane (Z = 165.1 mm). A long semicircular relief notch is cut into the body at mid‑height, running the full X length (see Features).

## Features
- **Bolt holes (12):** Ø8.43 mm, axis Y, through the 9.4 mm‑thick flange rim (opening on the +Y gasket face and into the cavity). All centred at Y = 122.3 mm. Pattern:
  - 4 on the top edge, Z = 322.4 mm, at X = 101.6 / 203.2 / 304.8 / 406.4 mm (101.6 mm spacing).
  - 4 on the bottom edge, Z = 7.8 mm, at X = 101.6 / 203.2 / 304.8 / 406.4 mm (101.6 mm spacing).
  - 2 on the left edge, X = 10.8 mm, at Z = 50.33 mm and Z = 280.55 mm.
  - 2 on the right edge, X = 497.2 mm, at Z = 50.33 mm and Z = 279.87 mm.
  These 12 through‑holes are the only through‑passages in the part.
- **Perimeter bolt pads (12):** each bolt hole sits in a raised, rounded pad on the flange rim (semicircular pads on the top/bottom edges, corner pads at the four corners); the flange edge is relieved (scalloped) between adjacent pads. Pad outer round Ø63.5 mm (convex), pad‑to‑flange blend Ø38.1 mm (concave).
- **Mid‑height relief notch (1):** a concave full cylinder Ø101.6 mm, axis X, running the full 508.0 mm length, centred at Z = 165.1 mm and cut in from the cavity side; it appears as the large semicircular notch in the left/right views.
- **Bowl bottom (crown):** a large convex cylindrical surface Ø254.0 mm (axis X) forming the outer bottom, with the inner cavity ceiling a matching concave cylindrical surface Ø228.6 mm (axis X); the bowl ends are blended (convex Ø127.0 mm). The cavity is a deep rectangular pocket with rounded internal corners, open on the +Y side.
- **Side‑wall relief:** a shallow concave cylindrical relief Ø228.6 mm / convex Ø127.0 mm pair shapes the bowl side profile seen in the side views.

## Edges and finish
- R25.4 mm convex rounds on the four outer flange corners and at the bowl ends (8 faces).
- R38.1 mm convex rounds (2 faces) and R25.4 mm convex rounds (2 faces) on the pad/bowl transitions.
- R25.4 mm concave fillets (2 faces) at the bowl‑to‑flange junction; R12.7 mm concave fillets (4 faces) and R6.35 mm concave fillet (1 face) at the notch and pad transitions.
- R38.1 mm concave (12 faces) and R63.5 mm convex (12 faces) rounds defining the perimeter bolt pads; small R12.7 mm concave (2 faces) and R25.4 mm convex (2 faces) partial rounds at the pad edges.
- Remaining edges (flange rim edges, cavity opening) are sharp.
