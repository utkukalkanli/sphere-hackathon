# C‑shaped spider mounting body

**Type / function:** Main structural body (spider) of a tubing assembly – a C‑shaped (horseshoe) frame that provides a large central cylindrical opening plus bossed and tabbed mounting/bore features.

**Envelope:** 328.0 x 169.5 x 333.23 mm (X x Y x Z); volume 5298.6 cm^3; 26 through‑passages.

## Overall shape
The part is a single solid C‑shaped (horseshoe) block whose plane of symmetry of the "C" is the X–Z plane; the thickness of the C is in Y (169.5 mm). The closed back of the C is the flat vertical face at –X, the two rounded lobes sit at the +Z and –Z extremes, and two rectangular mounting arms project in +X at the top and bottom of the opening. A large cylindrical opening with axis Y forms the inner curve of the C and is open to the +X face; the back (+Y) face carries a large concentric cylindrical housing/recess. The body is built up as a thick plate (the C web) with added lobes, arms, bosses and tabs. The whole part is mirror‑symmetric about the horizontal mid‑plane Z = 166.6 mm (every feature at Z has a mate at 333.23 − Z); it is *not* symmetric in X or Y.

## Features
- **Central C opening / back housing (axis Y):** a large cylindrical cutout whose inner wall is a concave cylindrical surface of dia 160, open to the +X face; on the back (+Y) face a concentric cylindrical recess whose bore is a concave cylindrical surface of dia 90.
- **Lobes (2, top and bottom, mirror about Z = 166.6):** each lobe carries (a) a vertical obround through‑slot with axis Y; (b) two through‑holes of dia 30 with axis X, one at Y = 55.0 (centres Z = 39.11 bottom / 294.11 top) and one at Y = 147.0 (centres Z = 26.36 bottom / 306.86 top); the Y = 147.0 hole is drilled through a dia 45 cylindrical boss (axis X, length 145.5) that protrudes on the back of the lobe.
- **Mounting arms (2, at +X, top and bottom):** rectangular tabs extending in +X; each has a dia 16 through‑hole, axis Y, at (305.5, 60.0, 98.11) and (305.5, 60.0, 235.11) (hole length 120), and a shallow dia 25 recess, axis Y (depth ≈ 1), at (272.0, 119.5, 90.61) / (272.0, 119.5, 242.61).
- **Left‑face bosses (2, at –X):** dia 18 cylindrical bosses, axis X, length 35, at (22.5, 60.0, 74.61) and (22.5, 60.0, 258.61), each with a coaxial dia 8 through‑hole (length 35).
- **Vertical stepped bores (2, axis Z):** through‑bores spanning the full 333.23 height, stepped dia 13 / coaxial dia 5, at (117.0, 147.0, 166.61) and (237.5, 147.0, 166.61) (centre spacing 120.5 in X); they sit in small tabs that protrude in +Y and reach the top and bottom edges.
- **Small dia 4 holes, axis Y (4, blind, length 19–20):** at (78.17, 109.0, 136.61), (78.17, 109.0, 196.61), (272.0, 109.5, 90.61) and (272.0, 109.5, 242.61).
- **Face counterbores/pockets (axis Y):** concave cylindrical pockets of dia 26 (four) and dia 28 (two) on the front/back faces around selected holes.

## Edges and finish
- Convex rounds R2 (18 edges), R5 (3) and R10 (6); concave fillets R2 (4), R20 (4) and R21 (2) – the R20/R21 fillets break the corners where the arms and lobes meet the C web.
- Toroidal fillets of minor radius R2 (22) and R5 (2).
- Conical chamfers of 20° half‑angle (3) and 70° half‑angle (3) at selected bore/boss ends.
- Remaining edges sharp.
